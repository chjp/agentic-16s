PROJECT GOAL: a machine-learning model that tells IBD from healthy using a stool 16S test. Only
studies that sequenced human stool can contribute.

INCLUSION CRITERIA (ALL must be met):
1. Studies human participants with inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn's disease and/or ulcerative colitis, including IBD-unclassified)
2. Sequences human stool (fecal) samples. Studies that also sequence other sample types qualify for their stool samples.
3. Characterizes the microbial community by 16S rRNA gene amplicon sequencing (studies that also do shotgun metagenomics are fine if 16S data is included)
4. Original research article that collected and sequenced new samples
Healthy controls in the same study are preferred but not required: stool from IBD patients alone (for example two treatment arms) is still included, because the pooled database compares it with controls from other studies.

EXCLUSION CRITERIA (any one leads to exclusion):
1. Animal models or in vitro studies only (no human samples sequenced)
2. No stool samples: only mucosal biopsy, surgical or fistula tissue, saliva or other oral samples, blood, or other non-stool sites
3. Reviews, meta-analyses, protocols, editorials, or opinion pieces
4. Microbiome measured only by shotgun metagenomics, qPCR, culture, metabolomics, or other non-16S methods
5. Re-analysis of public datasets only, with no new samples collected
6. Case reports or studies with fewer than 10 participants
7. IBD is not a studied group (e.g. irritable bowel syndrome, colorectal cancer, or celiac disease only)

If the abstract does not say how the microbiome was measured, or does not say which sample type was
sequenced, choose UNCERTAIN rather than EXCLUDE.
