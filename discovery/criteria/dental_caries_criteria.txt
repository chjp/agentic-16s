INCLUSION CRITERIA (ALL must be met):
1. Studies human saliva samples (not other oral sites like plaque, tongue, gingival crevicular fluid, or buccal swabs)
2. Primary focus on dental caries (cavities, tooth decay) as the main outcome or disease of interest
3. Uses 16S rRNA gene sequencing technology (not shotgun metagenomics, metabolomics, proteomics, or culture-based methods)
4. Reports microbiome/microbial community analysis (not just single pathogen detection)
5. Original research article with data collection (not reviews, meta-analyses, or opinion pieces)

EXCLUSION CRITERIA (any one leads to exclusion):
1. Animal models or in vitro studies (exclude non-human studies)
2. Primary focus on other oral diseases (periodontitis, gingivitis, oral cancer, candidiasis, etc.)
3. Studies of dental treatments or interventions without baseline microbiome data
4. Only plaque or biofilm samples (no saliva component)
5. Purely computational or methods development papers without novel biological findings
6. Case reports or small pilot studies with n < 10 samples
